MiaraOS

Support

MiaraOS is a calm, private log of feeds, diapers and sleep, shared with everyone who cares for your baby.

Need help?
Email noah@presler.me. Include your device and what you were doing, and we’ll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I invite a caregiver?
Parents can open More → Invite a caregiver, choose a role and share the single-use code. It expires in 7 days.

Is my data private?
Yes. Only people in your household can see your records, and there are no ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.

Can I try it without an account?
Yes. Tap “Explore with demo data” on the sign-in screen. Demo data is synthetic and stays on your phone.

Can I log with Siri, Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini?
Yes. Say “Hey Siri, log with Miara”, then what happened. To use an AI assistant, add a custom connector (in Claude), app (in ChatGPT developer mode) or custom app (in Gemini) with the address https://miara.presler.me/mcp, sign in to MiaraOS and choose Allow. The app’s More → Siri and assistants screen has the steps.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open More → Delete account. It shows exactly what will be removed before you confirm. If you’re the only parent in a household, the household and its records are deleted too. If you can’t sign in, email noah@presler.me.

MiaraOS shows general reference ranges from published pediatric guidance. It isn’t medical advice; talk with your pediatrician about your baby’s health.
